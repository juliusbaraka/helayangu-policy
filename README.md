# Privacy Policy for HelaYangu

HelaYangu is a personal budgeting application. 

### Data Privacy & Storage
* **Zero Server Storage:** HelaYangu does not operate any external databases or servers. All budgeting entries are stored locally on your device.
* **Google Drive Integration:** The application requests access to Google Drive solely to upload backup files and restore them at your direction. 
* **Developer Access:** The developer of HelaYangu has no access to your files, budget configurations, or personal account details. All client-to-cloud interactions occur securely inside your private Google sandbox.
